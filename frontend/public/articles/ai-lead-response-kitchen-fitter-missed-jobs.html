Priya runs a two-person kitchen and bathroom fitting business just outside Milton Keynes. Most enquiries land the same way: someone fills in her website's quote form at 9pm, then waits. Priya was out fitting units all day, so she replied in the evening, sometimes the next one. She assumed a day's wait was normal. It wasn't. While she finished a job, three other fitters had already replied to the same enquiry.

The enquiry that was already gone

Being busy on the tools was quietly costing her the next job

Priya's problem wasn't her work or her prices, it was the gap between a prospect hitting send and hearing back. Made Smarter UK found the average business now takes 47 hours to reply to a new enquiry, slower than a decade ago, and that 73% of leads are lost to exactly that kind of delay.

Priya's site got roughly 25 quote-form submissions a month. She was only getting the first proper conversation with about a third of them, the rest had booked another fitter, usually whoever replied that same evening. At an average kitchen job worth £6,000, losing four or five of those a month was quietly costing her £25,000 to £30,000 a year.

A two-minute reply changed which jobs she won

The fix was speed, not a bigger sales team

Priya didn't hire anyone. She connected an AI reply tool to her website form and WhatsApp number instead. The moment someone enquires, it sends a friendly reply within minutes, asks the room, rough budget, and timing, then books her next free site-visit slot. Anything urgent or unusual gets flagged straight to her phone.

Three months in, Priya gets that first conversation with closer to two-thirds of enquiries instead of a third, worth two extra kitchen jobs a month on average. The tool costs under £40 a month. One extra job covers more than a year of it.

Quick Answers

The questions small business owners ask most

Won't an instant AI reply put off a customer about to spend thousands on their kitchen?

Most don't mind, as long as it's useful and quick rather than a generic loop. The alternative was silence until evening, and a fast reply beats that easily.

What happens to enquiries that need Priya's judgement straight away?

Anything urgent, unusual, or price-sensitive gets flagged to her phone with the details already captured, so she only steps in where it matters.

How much does a tool like this typically cost a small business?

Most AI lead-response tools for a business this size run £20 to £60 a month, well under the value of a single job in most trades.

Does this only work for enquiries that come through a website form?

No. The same approach works for Facebook and Instagram messages, WhatsApp, and even missed calls, anywhere a prospect is waiting for a reply.

Why this matters for you

For a small trade or service business, the gap between getting an enquiry and replying to it is one of the few places where speed, not price or quality, decides who wins the job. A sole trader or two-person team can't always answer the moment a form comes in, but a cheap AI tool can.
