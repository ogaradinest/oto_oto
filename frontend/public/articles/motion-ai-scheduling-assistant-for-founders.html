What Motion Actually Does

An AI planner, not just a shared calendar

Motion isn't a to-do list with reminders bolted on. Give it your tasks, deadlines, and meetings, and its AI decides what happens when, blocking focus time around calls automatically instead of you slotting everything in by hand. When a meeting lands or a deadline shifts, it replans the rest of the day itself.

Take a solo SaaS founder pre-launch. Most mornings she lost 20 minutes moving tasks by hand after an investor call got added overnight. She loaded her task list into Motion one afternoon; it now blocks two hours of coding time before lunch by default and reslots everything else whenever a call appears, saving her close to five hours a week she used to spend just rearranging her own day.

Worth It Before You've Hired Anyone?

The gap between doing everything yourself and affording an assistant

Motion starts around $19 a month for a single user — roughly the value of one hour of the admin time NerdWallet UK's survey suggests owners lose each week. Reclaim, cheaper from about $8 a month, takes a lighter approach: it protects focus time and habits inside a calendar you still largely manage, rather than building the whole day. For a founder juggling competing priorities with no one to delegate to, Motion's fuller auto-scheduling tends to save more time; for someone who mainly needs protected deep-work blocks, Reclaim is the cheaper fit.

Neither tool replaces judgement. Motion still needs an honest task list and real deadlines — feed it a vague list and it schedules vague work. And it won't answer client emails or sit in on a call for you. What it removes is the daily admin of managing the plan itself, which Doodle's research puts at over 150 hours a year in manual meeting organising alone.

Is an AI planner worth it before I've even hired anyone?

Often yes — solo founders juggling product, sales, and admin lose the most time to manual rescheduling, and Motion starts from about $19 a month, far below what an assistant costs.

What's the actual difference between Motion and Reclaim?

Motion actively builds and rebuilds your schedule around tasks and deadlines. Reclaim, from about $8 a month, mainly protects existing focus time and habits within a calendar you still manage.

Does it replace needing a virtual assistant?

No — it won't answer emails or handle calls. It removes the daily admin of replanning your own calendar, narrowing the gap until you can afford to delegate more.
