The admin that eats the week

Where Priya's hours were actually going

Priya isn't unusual. UK small business owners spend an average of 11 hours a week on admin and finance tasks — nearly six working days a month — compared with just 3.6 days on sales and growth, according to the Amex SME Business Barometer. For a service business, a big chunk of that admin is meetings: the call itself, then the write-up, then chasing everyone to confirm what was actually decided.

For Priya, that meant roughly five hours a week gone before she'd done any billable work. Worse, her notes were inconsistent — sometimes a tidy list, sometimes just memory three days later — so two clients in the same month got different versions of what they'd agreed. One assumed a website redesign was starting in March; Priya's notes said April. That single mix-up nearly ended an £1,800-a-month retainer.

Turning the call into a ready-made brief

What changed once the AI joined the meeting

In January, Priya added an AI meeting assistant — she uses Fireflies.ai, though Otter and Fathom work similarly — that joins each video call automatically, transcribes it in real time, and emails a summary with action items within minutes of hanging up. She no longer types anything during the call; she just talks to the client and lets the tool catch the detail.

The habit that actually changed her week: she reads the summary once, fixes anything the AI mis-heard (client names and numbers still need a human check), and forwards it to the client that same afternoon instead of three days later. Clients started replying "yes, that's exactly it" instead of adding corrections — because they were seeing their own words back, not Priya's memory of them.

Quick Answers

The questions small business owners ask most

Is it legal to record client meetings with an AI notetaker in the UK?

Yes, but only with consent — UK GDPR requires you to tell everyone on the call before you start recording. A quick heads-up at the top of the meeting covers you.

Will it understand British accents and industry jargon?

Most tools handle UK accents well in 2026, but accuracy still varies by provider and gets shakier with niche technical terms — run one real call before switching your whole workflow over.

What does an AI meeting notetaker cost for a small team?

Most providers offer a usable free tier for one user; team plans with unlimited transcription typically run somewhere in the £10-£20 per user, per month range.

Does this replace checking my own notes?

No — treat the AI summary as a strong first draft. It still occasionally mishears a name, a number, or a date, so a 60-second read-through before you send it stays essential.

Why this matters for you

This matters more for a five-person consultancy than for a 500-person firm, because Priya doesn't have a PA or an ops manager to absorb the admin — every hour she spends typing notes is an hour she isn't billing or pitching. For a small business, that's not a productivity nicety — it's the gap between keeping a retainer and losing one.
