What Lovable actually does

You describe the app, it writes and ships it

Open Lovable and you're typing into a chat box, not a code editor. Describe what you want, say a booking page where customers pick a slot and pay a deposit, and it writes the working app behind the scenes, database and payment hookup included. Keep chatting to tweak colours, add a field, or connect it to Stripe. No terminal, no Git, no developer on the payroll.

Picture a first-time founder with an idea for a dog-walking booking service. Over one weekend they describe the booking flow and a simple admin view to Lovable, test it with five friends, and fix what breaks, before spending a penny on a 'proper' build. That's the pitch: validate the idea before committing real money.

What it costs, and where it still needs you

Credits, not seats, and a plan before you'll need a developer anyway

Lovable's free plan gives a handful of credits a day to try it. Pro runs about $25 a month, less billed annually, for 100 monthly credits that scale up. Business sits around $50 a month and adds team features. Most small, early builds fit comfortably inside Pro.

The part nobody advertises: AI-written code still needs a human eye before real money or customer data touches it, especially payments, logins and personal details. Treat Lovable as a fast way to a working prototype, not your permanent engineering team once you have paying customers and more to lose.

Quick Answers

The questions small business owners ask most

Do I need to know how to code to use Lovable?

No. You describe what you want in plain English and it writes and wires up the app itself.

How much does Lovable cost for a small project?

Free to try with a handful of daily credits. Paid plans start around $25 a month, which covers most early-stage builds.

Is an app built with AI safe to launch for real customers?

Treat it as a strong first version. Have someone review the code before customer payments or personal data pass through it.

What's the difference between Lovable and hiring a developer?

Lovable gets you a working prototype in days for the price of a subscription. A custom-built UK MVP typically costs £30,000 to £60,000 and takes ten to sixteen weeks.

Why this matters for you

Building a business before it exists on paper has one blocker: you need software but can't afford a developer until you have customers, and you can't get customers without the software. Tools like Lovable break that loop, letting you test demand before paying a developer's day rate to find out.
