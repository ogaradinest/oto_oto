The shoebox-and-spreadsheet problem

Why the smallest expenses eat the most time

Take a six-person marketing consultancy, call it the norm rather than the exception. Every month, the office manager spends the better part of a day chasing five colleagues for missing receipts, typing figures into a spreadsheet, then cross-checking each line against the bank feed to make sure nothing's been claimed twice. None of it is difficult work. All of it is slow, and it's the kind of task that gets pushed to the end of the day, which means it gets done tired.

Multiply that across a year and Pleo's breakdown puts the processing cost alone, before you even count errors, at over £4,000 annually for a business of ten people. For a team of six, that's still real money spent on data entry, not client work.

What changed when the AI did the matching

Same receipts, a different starting point

The fix wasn't a new accounting system, it was a different first step. Staff now photograph a receipt the moment they get it, and an AI tool reads the merchant, date, amount and likely category, then matches it against the corresponding bank line automatically. The office manager's job shifted from typing everything in to reviewing what the AI flagged as unusual, a duplicate claim, a receipt with no matching transaction, an amount that looked off.

The month-end job that used to take the better part of a day now takes under two hours, most of it spent on the handful of exceptions the software couldn't resolve on its own. Nobody's chasing WhatsApp screenshots anymore either, since the photo goes straight into the system from the person's phone.

Quick Answers

The questions small business owners ask most

Is it safe to connect AI expense software to our business bank account?

Reputable tools use read-only, bank-level encrypted feeds, similar to what your accounting software already uses. You're not handing anyone your login details.

Do we need to switch accounting software to try this?

No. Most expense tools plug into whatever you already use, Xero, QuickBooks, or a spreadsheet, rather than replacing it.

How much does this cost for a small team?

Entry-level plans typically run a few pounds per user per month, well under what manual processing costs once you count the time involved.

Will it catch duplicate or fraudulent claims?

It flags duplicates and mismatched receipts automatically, but a person still needs to review and approve before anything gets paid out.

Why this matters for you

For a small business, the value isn't the few minutes saved on any single receipt, it's what disappears from the calendar: the monthly chase for missing paperwork, the evening spent double-checking a spreadsheet, the errors that slip through because someone was rushing. None of that needs new accounting software or a finance hire, just a tool that reads a photo faster and more accurately than a tired person typing at 6pm.
