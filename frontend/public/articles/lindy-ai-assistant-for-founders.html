What Lindy actually does

An AI teammate for your inbox and calendar, not another chatbot

Lindy connects to your email and calendar and builds no-code AI agents that triage and draft messages, schedule and reschedule meetings, sit in on calls to write up notes and action items, and update your CRM afterwards — across more than 100 connected apps, triggered automatically or on demand.

Take a two-person founding team pre-seed: one of them lost an hour most mornings sorting overnight email and moving calls after a late investor reply. They set Lindy to triage the inbox into three folders and auto-reschedule anything clashing with deep-work blocks. Within a week that hour had shrunk to ten minutes of checking Lindy's summary.

Worth it before you've hired anyone?

Weighing $50-100 a month against doing it all yourself

Lindy's Plus plan runs $49.99 a month for two inboxes; Pro adds browser automation and three inboxes for $99.99. There's no free tier, just a seven-day trial. Compared with a part-time human assistant — typically £2,000-3,000 a month in the UK — it's a fraction of the cost, though it's also narrower: Lindy handles structured, repeatable admin well, but a founder still has to make the judgement calls it can't.

It's also not the only option — Motion focuses on auto-building your daily schedule around tasks and deadlines, while Lindy covers more ground across email, notetaking, and CRM updates. For a founder with a genuinely messy inbox and no support staff, that broader coverage tends to pay off fastest.

Quick questions

What founders ask before connecting an AI agent to their inbox

Is Lindy worth paying for before I've hired any staff?

Often yes for a founder buried in email and rescheduling — at $49.99 a month it's far cheaper than part-time human support, though it won't make judgement calls for you.

What's the difference between Lindy and an AI scheduling tool like Motion?

Motion focuses on auto-building your daily schedule from tasks and deadlines. Lindy covers more ground — email triage, meeting notes, and CRM updates — as a broader admin agent.

Is my data safe with an AI agent reading my inbox?

Lindy states it adheres to GDPR, SOC 2, HIPAA and PIPEDA standards, but any founder connecting an AI tool to email or a CRM should check the vendor's specific data-handling terms first.

How much admin time can I realistically expect back?

Knowledge workers using production AI agents recover a median 6.4 hours a week per seat, per McKinsey and Slack's 2026 research — expect a meaningful chunk of your admin time, not all of it.
