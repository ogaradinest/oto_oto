The questions small business owners ask most

Is a Gamma-made deck actually good enough to raise money with?

As a first draft, yes — investors care more about clear thinking and honest numbers than slide design. Treat it as scaffolding you fact-check and rewrite in your own voice, not the finished pitch.

Do investors mind if a deck was built with AI?

Most don't, and many assume it now. What they mind is generic content, unverified numbers, or a founder who doesn't understand their own slides.

Can I import my existing business plan?

Yes — paste in your written plan or notes and Gamma's AI restructures it into a slide-by-slide deck instead of a blank page.
